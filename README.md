Flagship projects

1. RGRHCL Housing Scheme MIS & Beneficiary Monitoring

Problem: Management needs to track movement from applications and sanctions to house completion, identify pending stages and review district performance.

Dataset: Experience-backed reporting context with privacy-safe synthetic district, scheme, stage and fund-release figures.

Workflow: Define housing stages → validate records → model district/scheme progress → calculate sanctions and completions → flag pending cases.

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Advanced Excel and government MIS controls.

Deliverables: Housing overview, progress funnel, completion-rate gauge, district heatmap, stage analysis, fund-release treemap and exportable details.

Decisions supported: District follow-up, sanction-to-completion monitoring, processing review and pending-case prioritisation.

2. Finance Budget, Expense & Forecasting Analytics

Problem: Finance teams need a consistent view of budget utilisation, material variance, vendor exposure, ageing and expected year-end spend.

Dataset: 4,320 synthetic budget rows, 5,000 expenses, 12 departments and 120 vendors.

Model: Budget and Expense facts with Department, Category, Vendor and Calendar dimensions.

Measures: Budget, actual, favourable/unfavourable variance, utilisation, outstanding amount and run-rate forecast.

Deliverables: Financial overview, variance decomposition, payment ageing, category/vendor analysis, exception thresholds and CSV detail export.

Decisions supported: Cost control, department review, vendor concentration follow-up and forecast adjustment.

3. Workforce, Attrition & Recruitment Analytics

Problem: HR needs to understand workforce composition, attrition patterns and hiring conversion without confusing association with causation.

Dataset: 1,000 synthetic employees, 2,000 candidates and 10 departments.

Model: Employee and Recruitment facts with Department and Calendar dimensions.

Measures: Headcount, tenure, salary, attrition rate, offer acceptance, joining conversion and time to hire.

Deliverables: Workforce overview, attrition analysis, recruitment funnel, segmentation and drill-through details.

Decisions supported: Workforce planning, retention investigation, recruiter performance and hiring-process improvement.
